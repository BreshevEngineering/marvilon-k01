# K01 P003 ↔ P007 — manufacturing and assembly baseline

## Functional architecture
P003 and P007 are both EN 1.4404 / AISI 316L.

Final geometry:
- P003 rear weld collar: OD16 over final 5.00 mm.
- P007 total length: 35.00 mm.
- P007 front/root region: OD16 / ID14.1.
- P007 thin magnetic wall: OD10 / ID9.4 / t0.30.
- P007 rear closure: integral blind end; no separate rear-cap weld.

The old 5 mm telescopic P007 sleeve is removed.

## Joint
P003 rear face and P007 front face form a circumferential butt joint at common
OD16. The joint is a **static containment weld**. It is not a locating feature.

Drawing/process note:
`CONTINUOUS 360° FULL-PENETRATION BUTT WELD, LEAK-TIGHT.
PROCESS/WPS TO BE QUALIFIED. NO INTERNAL WELD PROTRUSION INTO THE
P007 ID14.1 CLEARANCE ENVELOPE.`

Do not specify autogenous laser, GTAW/TIG or filler as the sole released process
until representative coupons and actual 316L heat chemistry support the WPS.

## Recommended P007 machining route
For prototype/small-volume production:
1. Start from certified 316L bar/tube stock with machining allowance.
2. Machine thick OD16 root and rough OD10 body.
3. Drill/precision-bore ID9.4 to controlled depth, leaving the integral 1.00 mm
   blind rear wall.
4. Finish ID/root transition.
5. Finish OD10 thin wall using low cutting force and supported workholding.
6. Inspect OD, ID, wall thickness/ovality at multiple clock positions.
7. Protect the 0.30 mm thin zone during all later fixturing.

No weld is permitted at the blind rear end.

## Sealed-cartridge assembly sequence before P003↔P007 welding
1. Clean/degrease/passivate machined containment parts as required by process.
2. Assemble P003 internal guide/stop hardware and P001 rod in the approved order.
3. Fully seat P006; verify required P002 axial float.
4. Assemble P008 follower to P001 without M4 bottoming.
5. Install B001/P013 according to the released follower-retention process.
6. Functionally verify free 10.00 mm hard-stop travel before containment closure.
7. Slide open-front P007 over P008/B001 from the rear.
8. Seat P007 front face against P003 rear weld face; fixture coaxially on
   non-critical robust surfaces. The weld shall not be used for alignment.
9. Execute qualified 360° containment weld.
10. After cooling, verify free IN↔OUT motion, no follower/can rubbing, axial stop
    function, and P007 OD10 ovality/runout.
11. Install external P015 coil/bobbin after containment welding.
12. Perform integral helium leak test as production-release evidence.

Because the P003↔P007 weld is permanent, the internal follower/magnet is not a
field-service item. Service replacement baseline is the sealed cartridge/module,
not cutting and rewelding P007 in the field.
