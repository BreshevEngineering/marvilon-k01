# K01 P003 ↔ P007 — serviceable containment decision

The permanent P003↔P007 weld is **rejected as the production baseline** because
the module must be dismantlable and maintainable. The existing promoted
P003/P007 geometry remains a validated structural prototype baseline, not the
final service interface.

## Recommended removable architecture

P003 rear:
- service flange OD34 × 5;
- male locating pilot Ø16 g6 × 1.50 protrusion;
- pilot internal clearance bore ≥ Ø12.0 so current P006 head Ø11.5 passes;
- static face O-ring groove candidate ID17.8 / OD21.8 / depth1.20;
- 4× M3-6H on PCD28, full thread ≥5.0.

P007 front:
- service flange OD34 × 3;
- Ø16 H7 counterbore depth1.70;
- 0.20 nominal pilot-end clearance;
- 4× Ø3.4 THRU on PCD28;
- existing ID14.1 region and all downstream OD10/ID9.4 magnetic geometry retained.

Seal candidate:
- 18×1.5 FKM75 static face seal;
- FFKM remains an option if measured-gas compatibility demands it.

Clamp:
- 4× M3×8 A4-70 low-profile/button-head screws.
- Screws clamp only. Ø16 H7/g6 pilot defines radial location.
- Metal-to-metal flange seating defines axial position and controls O-ring squeeze.
- Torque remains OPEN until fastener finish/anti-galling method and final O-ring
  compound are frozen.

## Why four M3 screws are not structural overkill

At +0.20 bar, the measured/analytical axial pressure resultant is only ~3.123 N.
The fasteners are sized primarily for uniform static face-seal compression,
repeatability and service robustness, not pressure strength.

## Service sequence

1. Disconnect P015 leads.
2. Slide the one-piece P015 bobbin rearward off the OD10 can.
3. Remove the four P003↔P007 screws.
4. Withdraw P007 axially.
5. P006 and P008/B001/P013 become accessible.
6. Replace the static O-ring during scheduled service unless a validated reuse
   procedure exists.
7. Reassemble on the Ø16 pilot and tighten screws in a cross sequence.

No weld cutting or rewelding is required.
